Editorial Policy

How we research, source, fact-check, correct, and update what we publish — and what we will never do.

Last Updated: 23 November 2026

What we aim to do

Our Editorial Mission

BlogSphere publishes practical, in-depth guides across five categories: Technology, Business, Home Decor, Health & Fitness, and Make Money Online. Our aim is simple - a reader should finish an article knowing something useful they didn't know before, and be able to trust what they read. We write for readers first, not for search engines, and we would rather publish less than publish something we can't stand behind.

Research and Sourcing

Every article starts with research, not with a blank page and an opinion. We check primary sources wherever possible - official announcements, company statements, government guidance, and the original report or study behind a claim - rather than repeating what another blog said about it.

Where we cite a statistic, a date, a price, a rule, or a threshold, we aim to name the source and link to it so a reader can check it themselves. Where a figure comes from a single source, we say so. Where sources disagree, we say that too, rather than picking the one that suits the article.

We prefer authoritative sources: official government pages, regulators, established news organisations, and the companies or organisations directly involved. We treat anonymous or single-source claims with caution and label them as unverified when we cannot confirm them.

Fact-Checking

Before an article goes live, we check its factual claims - names, dates, numbers, quotes, and the steps in any how-to guide. Claims about health, money, tax, or legal matters get extra scrutiny, because a wrong figure there can cost a reader real money or affect their health.

We do not invent claims, statistics, sources, or quotes. If we cannot verify a fact, we either leave it out, soften the wording to match what we can actually support, or flag it clearly as unverified. We do not present rumour as fact, and we do not present an estimate as a measured figure.

Where an article discusses a developing story, we state what is confirmed, what is alleged, and what is still unverified, and we date the information so a reader can tell how fresh it is.

Editorial Independence

Our editorial judgment is independent of our advertisers and affiliate partners. Advertising and affiliate links do not influence which topics we cover or what we say about them. A product we link to with an affiliate link is recommended because we think it is worth recommending, not because it pays a commission - and we say so in the article when an affiliate link is present. See our Terms & Conditions for our affiliate disclosure and our Privacy Policy for how advertising cookies work.

We do not accept payment for positive coverage, and we do not publish paid or sponsored content without labelling it clearly as such.

Corrections

We make mistakes, and when we do, we fix them. If you spot something factually wrong in an article, tell us through our contact page or by email at admin@blogsphere.info - include the article title and link, what is wrong, and the correct information if you have it.

We check every correction report against a reliable source. Confirmed errors are corrected in the article, and where the error was significant, we note the correction in the article so readers can see what changed. We do not silently rewrite an article to hide an error.

Updates

We revisit older articles regularly, especially in fast-moving categories like Technology, where facts go stale quickly. When we update an article, we update its content to match the current facts; the original publish date is not changed. If an article is substantially rewritten, we say so in the article.

Articles that are no longer accurate and cannot be brought up to date are removed or clearly marked, rather than left to mislead readers.

Comments and Reader Contributions

Readers can comment on our articles. Comments are reviewed before they appear. We may remove comments that are spam, abusive, defamatory, or off-topic, and we do not publish comments that contain personal information about others. A comment represents its author, not BlogSphere.

Contact the Editorial Team

Questions about how we work, a correction, or a topic you think we should cover? Reach us through our contact page or by email at admin@blogsphere.info.
