    Business

    I made many mistakes in business. I under-priced, invested in ineffective marketing and took on the wrong jobs. Below I will give you what I have learned, so that you can avoid making these mistakes.

    Strategy

    Despite sounding complex, building a good strategy is really straightforward. Ask yourself a few questions: who is my target audience? What problem am I solving for them? Why should they choose me? Once you have clear answers to these questions, everything else becomes easier. I have written notes on pricing, planning, budgeting, recruitment and difficult decision making. I have also included advice that won’t necessarily make sense for a small team, as a lot of it is only suitable for large businesses with large budgets.

    Marketing

    It is a huge shame to create a great product but not be able to get people to hear about it. I have written notes on marketing using social media, email, SEO, content and branding. Overall the effective techniques aren’t too exciting. It’s very much trial and error. Going viral is rare and definitely shouldn’t be relied upon in any marketing plan. However, regular and helpful content is very effective.

    Growth

    Although growing a business can seem like a positive thing to do, it can bring new challenges. For example, with more customers comes more emails, issues to manage and people to please. With 3 people in a business things may run smoothly, but with 10 people it can become complicated. I have given tips on acquiring new customers, growing sustainably and understanding if staying small may be more beneficial. Not all businesses should be growing rapidly, doubling every year. Some of the best businesses I’ve seen are small, stable and quietly profitable.

    Productivity

    No matter how hard we try, sometimes it seems as though we get very little done in a day! I have provided tips on improving productivity. Improve meeting efficiency, reduce unecessary tasks, and use tools which improve productivity. I don’t have a perfect productivity system and if someone does, I certainly don’t trust them. Pick one of these ideas, and if it helps then great, if not, try something else!

    Who it is for

    This is for anyone running a business, working in a small team, freelancing, or wanting to start a business. No experience or qualifications are required, simply the desire to do a good job and a willingness to learn.

    I’m still learning! The business world can change quickly, and strategies that worked last year may not be as effective now. Please use the information below as a guideline rather than strict rules. Have a read through, see what topic relates most to the current challenges you have in business and pick up any tips that you can!
