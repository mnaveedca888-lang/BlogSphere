    Technology

    New tech updates are very common! Whether it’s new phone models, redesigns to apps, or new AI functions within companies, it seems like there is always something new to discover. We have included this section so that you can keep updated without getting overwhelmed.

    Latest Tech News

    We include the relevant articles. If a company has released a new product/updated something we’ll let you know how this affects you. This could be a major change, or just a minor update with lots of marketing. We also discuss larger scale changes in technology, such as data leaks, new regulations and changes in the operations of large tech companies. These can ultimately affect how you use something, and how much you pay for it.

    Product Reviews

    In product reviews, we aim to give you the answer to ‘should I buy this?’ We look at phones, laptops, headphones, smartwatches, cameras and general devices. We give you honest opinions on products. Despite having fantastic specifications, some products may not be pleasant to use. Alternatively, some devices may lack style but be very reliable. We discuss how long the battery lasts, comfort, price and value for money. If cheaper alternatives perform the same function as the product, we’ll let you know!

    Tips for Software

    Most people do not use all the functionality available on their devices. Within this section, we provide tips for general use. This includes quick shortcuts, difficult settings and useful applications. We also give solutions to problems you may have on your device, such as slowness, a full device or unreliable wi-fi. We give tips for Windows, Mac OS, Android and iOS. Instructions are simple to understand and do not assume knowledge of technical topics.

    Tech Trends

    There are certain trends in technology. Artificial Intelligence is now used in searches, emails, photos and working tools. Managing privacy online is becoming increasingly difficult. Smart devices, wearable devices and cloud services are now common in everyday life. We have written articles in a simple way and focused on how these trends affect regular users.

    Audience

    No prior technical knowledge is required. If you’re just interested in how things work, unsure what to buy, or are regularly getting the same issue within your tech, then this is the section for you. Even if you have plenty of knowledge, we hope that you’ll find a few things you didn’t know.

    Although tech is constantly changing, and nobody can know everything about it, having just a little curiosity and clear information makes it easy to keep up. Have a look through recent posts, and follow any topic which interests you!
