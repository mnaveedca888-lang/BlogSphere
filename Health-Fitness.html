    Healthy Habits

    Although I have had a stop-start approach to these healthy habits, I no longer view this negatively. This page aims to support people to implement healthy habits in their busy lives. Whether due to work, family commitments, exhaustion, or generally having a tough time, this page will help.

    Exercise

    No gym membership required! This section includes exercises for beginners, short workouts, bodyweight workouts to do at home and low impact walks. Tips and guidance on how to ease in, increase intensity, and know when to stop! These workouts are perfect if you haven’t done exercise in a while or never have!

    Eating Well

    There is so much conflicting advice out there about what we should/ shouldn’t be eating. To make it simple, try to mostly eat whole foods, include enough protein and vegetables, lots of water, and allow yourself to enjoy the things that make you happy. I also give tips on quick meals, shopping, label reading, and eating well on a budget. There are no hard and fast rules in this section, and no foods that are restricted.

    Weight Loss

    I often get asked about weight loss, however I want to be very considerate in giving this advice. In general, slow sustainable weight loss is more sustainable than fad diets. What is suitable for one person may not be suitable for another. Tips include good sleeping habits, regular movement in your day and mindful eating. However, I avoid quick fixes and ‘magic’ solutions. If you have a medical condition or previous history of unhealthy eating behaviours, please consult with a professional or doctor first.

    Recovery

    Recovery is very important. Without allowing muscles to recover, and if you are very tired, performance in exercise is not great. Tips include getting good quality sleep, stretches, mobility work, dealing with sore muscles and knowing when to take a rest day. Taking care of your body within your training is really important. Ignoring pain will generally make you spend longer in recovery.

    General Wellbeing

    It’s important to consider how well you’re managing in other aspects of your life, including stress levels, mood, energy, and postures. And in your evenings. Small habits to incorporate include a short walk after lunch, a calmer morning, less screen time at night, or checking in with how you are.

    Quick Note:

    I am not a medical professional. If you are unwell, pregnant, have an illness or are unsure if it’s safe for you to complete any activities, please consult with a medical professional.

    Who this is for

    This can be for complete beginners, busy parents, office workers, people returning to exercise, or anyone looking to feel a bit better in their body. You don’t need to be very fit, highly motivated, or perfect. Just start somewhere, and move forwards (slowly if need be).
