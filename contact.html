Before You Write

A few details help us answer faster. For a correction, include the article title and link, what is wrong, and the correct information if you have it. For a topic suggestion, tell us what you would like us to cover and why it would help readers. For anything else, include enough detail for the request to be understood — a one-line message without context is hard to act on.

Common Questions

How do I suggest a topic?

Use the form below and choose a clear subject line, such as “Topic suggestion: …”. Tell us the topic, who it would help, and anything it should cover. We read every suggestion, and popular requests shape what we write next.

I spotted a mistake in an article. What should I send?

Send the article title and link, the part that is wrong, and the correct information if you have it. We check every report against a reliable source and fix confirmed errors.

Do you accept guest posts or advertising?

Use the form to tell us what you have in mind, including what you would like to contribute or promote. We only accept things that fit the site and are genuinely useful to readers.

Will I get a reply?

We read every message. If your message needs an answer, we aim to reply as soon as we can. A clear subject line — and the article link for corrections — helps us answer faster.
