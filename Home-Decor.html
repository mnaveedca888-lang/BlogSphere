    Interior

    I’ve lived in several homes with less than perfect interiors. I’ve learned that even on a small budget you can create a beautiful interior by making small changes! Below are some ideas to help.

    Space Ideas

    Within any rooms (living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens and even just small areas within an apartment) there are many ideas to consider. Whether it be layout, colour schemes, lighting, rugs, display units or finishing touches – there is something for every space. Some of these ideas can be made with very little money (moving furniture or changing lamps), others may take more thought to implement. I’ve tried to indicate which ideas will take more planning to allow you to decide how much effort you wish to put into your interior.

    Comfortable Room

    Creating a comfortable room to live in is key to creating a wonderful home. This often involves using soft lights, having a blanket to hand and adding a few plants and seating that makes you reluctant to leave! However, this doesn’t mean that a room must be messy, nor does it have to match a certain style. In this section I’ve listed different ideas to make a space warmer. I’ve also included fabric choices, candles and organisation ideas. If you have a great looking space but just aren’t getting the warm feeling from it, this is a great place to start.

    Current Trends

    Not every trend is worth following. Some trends have become very popular very quickly and then disappear just as quickly. I tend to be cautious with interior design trends. I’ll include current trends in interior design (natural textures, warm neutral tones, strong statement walls and vintage items) and some ways to incorporate them without completely changing your interior. Whatever trend you choose to follow, aim to keep it if you still love it after 1 month. Otherwise it was just a trend!

    Functional Beauty

    Whilst creating a beautiful space in your home, practicality is key. It is important to have storage, space for your shoes, a workstation without taking over your dining table and furniture that can withstand children/pets. In this section I have included tips for budget friendly decoration, easily changeable decoration, tidying and organisation, making the most of a smaller space and simple DIY projects. None of these require specialist skills. As long as you can use a screwdriver and are patient, these ideas are accessible to everyone.

    Who this is for

    This post is perfect for anyone who has just moved into a new space and wants some ideas to get started, or maybe you have been living in your current space for a while and would like to give it a refresh. Or perhaps you just like looking at ideas on a lazy day. Regardless of why you are here, I’m glad you are! No previous knowledge or large budgets are needed.

    There are no rules to decorating your home as you wish! Whilst it may not look like it has come straight out of a magazine, as long as it feels like home to you, it’s perfect. Have a read through this post, and save any ideas you like. Feel free to skip any ideas you’re not interested in. Overall just enjoy!
