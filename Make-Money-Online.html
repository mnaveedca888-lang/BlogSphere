    Earning money online

    There is a large amount of misinformation and exaggerated claims on how to earn money online. Sometimes there are very high earnings shown in images, or expensive courses to buy. As it can be very overwhelming, this section aims to give realistic insight into how to earn money online. It tends to take time, trial and error, and many little steps.

    Ideas

    There are many genuine ways to make money online. Whether writing, designing, editing or doing book keeping, selling digital products, blogging, running a small shop, tutoring, or even providing services to local businesses (who may be new to the internet) are all good options. Within this section, each option will be discussed. I will include information on costs (whether starting costs are required), and whether they would suit certain people. Some require starting costs, but many only require time and a bit of learning. In this section, I have indicated whether they do or not, so that you are not caught out!

    Strategies for digital earning

    It is one thing to have an idea for making money online. For many people, getting it started is the tricky part. This section covers finding your first customers/clients, pricing, creating a portfolio, consistency and time management. It also includes some helpful tools to get started (invoicing, scheduling and basic marketing) – but I have kept this very brief. You certainly don’t need to pay for 10+ services to get started.

    Diversify your income

    It is very easy for single income streams to come to an end. Whether it be losing clients, policy changes on the platform, or a drop in traffic following an update, diversifying income streams gradually over time can be a good idea. This means having a major income stream to support yourself, as well as minor income streams growing in the background. This section gives tips on how to do this without overworking yourself, and knowing whether to invest more time into a side hustle or cut it out.

    Realistic Information

    I won’t make any exaggerated claims that you can become extremely rich in just 30 days. In general, it does take months of working to make money. Many attempts may also be unsuccessful. Common scams and poor advice are given in many places, so I will highlight these. Avoid any offers that ask you to pay a large amount of money for guaranteed returns. If it sounds too easy, it probably is! Be cautious with any money and do some research before committing to something!

    Who this is for

    This section is suitable for students, parents, people with full time jobs looking to make some extra money, or simply people considering self employment. You do not need any technical or business knowledge. However, patience, a little bit of curiosity, and realistic expectations are needed.

    As everyone has different circumstances, just take what works for you. Try just one small idea, and give it a fair go. From here, it generally tends to develop.
